I’m a data scientist by training and a problem solver at heart.

Over eight years at GoTo (Indonesia’s #1 tech company), I invented GoFood’s first recommendation engine (Patent SG 10201912472U), built real-time contextual-bandit pricing systems that increased gross take by 15%, and grew a data team that [doubled monthly ad revenue](https://aws1.campaignasia.com/article/goto-ad-revenue-soars-92-as-on-demand-business-turns-profitable/501420) to $2M USD.

I later led the modernisation of GoFood’s Search and Recommendations stack using vector retrieval, deep ranking and LLM-powered relevance, [driving record growth for the company](https://www.gotocompany.com/en/news/press/goto-beats-guidance-achieving-record-results-as-it-reports-2025-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-earnings#:~:text=AI%20has%20played%20a%20key%20role%20in%20driving%20the%20business%2C%20for%20example%20the%20Company%20has%20advanced%20its%20Search%20and%20Recommendation%20capabilities%2C%20increasing%20discovery%2C%20conversion%20rates%20and%20basket%20size.) through a 13.5% uplift in overall conversion. I also led 50+ data scientists through GoTo’s cloud migration ([one of the most complex in SEA](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/press-room/goto-and-alibaba-cloud-complete-cloud-migration?_p_lc=1#:~:text=Supporting%20One%20of,disruptive%20user%20experience.)), reducing infrastructure costs by 40%.

My work spans AI/ML, software/data engineering, experimentation, analytics and leadership. But the goal has always been the same: build systems that scale and create real value.
