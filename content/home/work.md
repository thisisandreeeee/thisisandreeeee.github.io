### The data geek

I have worked with some of the largest datasets in Southeast Asia at Gojek and Lazada. I build model-training pipelines with TensorFlow, Apache Beam, and Airflow, and production services with Go and Kubernetes on Google Cloud.

### The hacker

I solve problems with technology, sometimes within unreasonable deadlines. My teams have received more than $33,000 through hackathons, data-science competitions, and startup grants.

### The team player

I enjoy collaborating and get a rush from team sports. I served as soccer captain at my university college and won five medals, including two golds, at the World Dragon Boat Racing Championships in Florida.
